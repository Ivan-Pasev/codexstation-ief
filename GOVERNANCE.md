# Governance

CodexStation IEF uses a promotion model rather than direct experimental mutation of the public core.

## Planes

- Google Drive canonical working tree: continuity, manuscript, research state, distributions.
- Public repository: reviewed architecture, specifications, schemas, reference adapters, examples and releases.
- Private laboratory repository: experiments, local integration, unpublished adapters, promotion candidates.

## Promotion states

`EXPERIMENT -> VALIDATED -> HARDENED -> PUBLIC_CANDIDATE -> REVIEWED -> PROMOTED`

No experimental result becomes public canon solely because it works once.
